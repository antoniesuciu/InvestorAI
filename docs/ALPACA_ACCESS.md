## Provider-specific interface facts

These are published interface descriptions. The initial samples in [access validation](ALPACA_ACCESS_VALIDATION.md) establish only the listed observed behavior; they do not validate every interface condition. Provider responses must remain separate from the normalized market model.

| Stream | Documented contract and implementation consequence |
| --- | --- |
| Bars | `GET https://data.alpaca.markets/v2/stocks/bars`: symbol-keyed results, `timeframe=1Day`, inclusive `start`/`end`, explicit `feed`, `sort`, `adjustment`, `asof`, `page_token`. Limit 1–10,000 applies across **all symbols**, with symbol-first/time-second ordering. Follow `next_page_token` even after a short page; first-page absence of MSFT does not mean no MSFT history. [Reference](https://docs.alpaca.markets/us/reference/stockbars) |
| Daily timestamps | Daily trade aggregates use the New York calendar day; bar timestamp identifies the interval's **left edge**, not publication or close. Preserve it, convert through `America/New_York`, and keep actual observation/completion times separately. Provider trade-condition rules affect OHLCV; do not assert equivalence to an official exchange closing price or invent zero-volume bars. [Aggregation FAQ](https://docs.alpaca.markets/us/docs/market-data-faq) |
| Adjustments | `raw` is unadjusted; `split` adjusts price and volume; `dividend` adjusts price for cash dividends; `spin-off` adjusts price; `all` includes those methods. Combinations exist. No documented parameter freezes all adjustment knowledge at an arbitrary past date. `asof` maps symbol identity across name changes; it is **not** the adjustment anchor or InvestorAI knowledge cutoff. [Bars](https://docs.alpaca.markets/us/reference/stockbars) |
| Actions | `GET https://data.alpaca.markets/v1/corporate-actions`: separate pagination, limit 1–1,000 across symbols, filters/sort based on **process date**, not ex-date. Retain independent request interval/cursor, provider ID, dates and original subtype. `data_quality=complete` can exclude unprocessed incomplete records; processed records can still be incomplete. Alpaca disclaims guaranteed creation timing. Exhausting pages is not proof of a complete ex-date ledger. [Reference](https://docs.alpaca.markets/us/reference/corporateactions-1) |
| Action schema | Results group types under `corporate_actions` with `next_page_token`. Forward/reverse splits have `new_rate`/`old_rate`; cash dividends have `rate`, `special`, `foreign`; process/ex/record/payable dates are separate. Preserve original ratios and identifiers. Optional `currency` can be empty meaning USD **or unknown/inapplicable**, so defaulting blindly to USD is unsound. Unsupported stock dividends, spin-offs, mergers, name changes and other types must be retained/flagged or fail explicitly. [Provider-owned OpenAPI schema](https://github.com/alpacahq/cli/blob/main/api/specs/market-data-api.json), [official SDK models](https://github.com/alpacahq/alpaca-py/blob/master/alpaca/data/models/corporate_actions.py) |
| Calendar | `GET https://paper-api.alpaca.markets/v2/calendar` is on the Trading API host. The reference advertises 1970–2029 and early closes; inclusive start/end, `date_type=TRADING` versus `SETTLEMENT`. Returned date/open/close and optional session fields are distinct. Map New York local times explicitly, including DST; the calendar does not prove instrument listing life. [Reference](https://docs.alpaca.markets/us/reference/legacycalendar), [SDK Calendar model](https://github.com/alpacahq/alpaca-py/blob/master/alpaca/trading/models.py) |
| Instrument identity | Read-only `GET https://paper-api.alpaca.markets/v2/assets/{symbol_or_asset_id}` accepts symbol or asset ID (CUSIP also accepted for US equities), returns an Asset or 404. Provider ID, symbol, class, exchange and status are observed current metadata, not certified historical listing identity. Retain source evidence and avoid silently treating current exchange/status as true throughout past bars. [Reference](https://docs.alpaca.markets/us/reference/get-v2-assets-symbol_or_asset_id), [Asset model](https://github.com/alpacahq/alpaca-py/blob/master/alpaca/trading/models.py) |
| Authentication/errors | Individual Trading API uses `APCA-API-KEY-ID` and `APCA-API-SECRET-KEY` headers; Broker authentication is different. Reject 401/403/entitlement errors without feed or paid fallback. Bound retries for transient errors; 429 exposes `X-RateLimit-Limit`, `Remaining`, `Reset`. Credentials, authentication headers and sensitive account/error content must stay out of retained provenance; allowlisted endpoint/query metadata and safe response headers may support reproducibility. [Authentication](https://docs.alpaca.markets/us/docs/about-market-data-api), [response schema](https://github.com/alpacahq/cli/blob/main/api/specs/market-data-api.json) |

The reviewed provider-owned schemas and SDK branches are mutable; reverify or pin their revision before enabling a live adapter. SDK numeric types are not a requirement to adopt binary floats: parse original JSON financial numbers with Decimal and validate before normalization.

No reviewed contract guarantees that all corrections retain an event ID, supplies a complete cancellation/retraction history, or bounds how far back a correction can arrive. Treat these as unresolved revision-model conditions. Exhausted pagination proves traversal of a response set, not absence of undisclosed events or historical vintage completeness.

## Operating constraints and unresolved contracts

1. Record the applicable account/feed assumption and unresolved public wording. Historical SIP is explicitly selected, not an automatic replacement for empty IEX data. Stop on 401/403 or subscription refusal; do not upgrade or bypass controls.
2. Keep real raw/normalized history private in ignored storage under the user's current authorization. No definite permitted archive duration or post-termination right was established. Review applicable account/exchange terms when available and reassess retention if terms, account status or use changes; honor a specific applicable restriction rather than inventing a retention period. No external contact was made during this task.
3. Keep configured credentials local; preserve explicit free-feed selection and disable upgrades. Coordinate quotas separately for Market Data and Trading API requests; a per-test limiter is not an account-wide quota implementation. The Trading host is used only for allowlisted read-only calendar/asset requests, never account execution or order routes.
4. Validate actual AAPL/MSFT bars, adjustments/actions, date/session meaning and corrections against primary evidence. Preserve retrieval vintages; current snapshots cannot certify pre-collection availability. Process-date polling alone cannot certify action completeness or guarantee arbitrary old corrections.

Synthetic fixtures remain the deterministic regression surface; newly authorized live collection and saved evidence must be labeled real and kept outside Git. Neither fixture tests nor a small live sample certifies complete provider coverage, a survivorship-free universe or a licensed perpetual archive.

**User-reported fact:** a Basic paper account now exists and credentials are configured in ignored local configuration. **What this resolves:** credentials are no longer an assumed prerequisite. They cannot establish retention permission, subscriber classification or account-specific contractual terms.

The separate [authenticated access validation](ALPACA_ACCESS_VALIDATION.md) subsequently observed HTTP 200 on IEX and historical SIP for the 2024 sample. Neither exact feed-history boundaries nor broad completeness were measured, and technical SIP availability does not resolve its use entitlement.

**Public terms versus account agreements:** the [paper-only guide](https://docs.alpaca.markets/us/docs/paper-trading) still states global signup and IEX entitlement. [General terms](https://files.alpaca.markets/disclosures/library/TermsAndConditions.pdf) give positive personal/noncommercial-use evidence, subject to other applicable agreements. [Trading Account documentation](https://docs.alpaca.markets/us/docs/account-plans) distinguishes a paper-only account from separately opening a brokerage account. Consequently, the public brokerage customer agreement's restrictions must not be asserted automatically to govern this paper account; applicability remains unreviewed. The actual signup/subscriber agreements accepted by the user were not supplied or inspected.

The [public disclosures library](https://alpaca.markets/disclosures) lists Nasdaq/NYSE subscriber agreements but no separately identified paper-only archival license in its displayed agreement list. **Current conclusion:** the [historical API guide](https://docs.alpaca.markets/us/docs/historical-api) identifies charting/backtesting/strategy research as intended uses, the FAQ expressly describes free historical SIP access, personal-use terms provide positive evidence, and the user has now authorized private local persistence. This is a scoped engineering decision under the user's instruction, not a contractual opinion or proof of indefinite rights.
