# Financial and market data research

Research date: 2026-10-27. Workstream 1 supporting report for [DATA_SOURCES.md](../DATA_SOURCES.md). Recommendations are proposals for review, not authorization to integrate providers.

## Method and interpretation

Inspected current provider documentation, pricing, registration pages, and terms. Used web retrieval and, for Finnhub's JavaScript pricing page, a live Firecrawl scrape. No accounts were created, payment details supplied, dependencies installed, or authenticated API tests performed. A public Alpha Vantage demo request for earnings estimates returned a notice to obtain a free key, so it did not verify free-key entitlement. Provider claims about quality, completeness, and speed are not measured service guarantees.

**Recommend** means evidence supports the stated narrow use. **Conditional** means a specific entitlement, licensing, retention, or registration question remains; these candidates are excluded from an unconditional automated-data stack. **Exclude** applies to the proposed use, rather than declaring every possible use prohibited. A zero-price API and a permission to build an autonomous, persistent research dataset are separate requirements.

## Findings and proposed direction

1. Use SEC EDGAR as the primary US company reporting source. It offers unusually strong provenance and free programmatic access; normalization and historical availability still require careful work.
2. Do not assume a global, long-history, survivorship-aware, point-in-time stock database can be assembled from one free API. None of the reviewed candidates establishes that complete capability under all project constraints.
3. Price-data choice remains conditional. Twelve Data Basic merits a bounded personal noncommercial pilot after checking history and permitted caching; Alpaca merits a separate eligibility and retention check. Massive's default display-only terms and Tiingo Starter's nonpersistent-storage rule defeat unconditional automated archival recommendations despite attractive free pricing.
4. Treat external consensus forecasts as an optional gap. Forecasts generated locally from reported evidence can proceed without them, clearly labeled as our projections.

## Licensing and point-in-time findings

### Primary filings

The SEC API aggregates standard, entity-wide XBRL concepts and omits custom-taxonomy and non-entity-wide details; full filings remain necessary for segment and unusual disclosures. The Frames API chooses last-filed facts matching a calendar period, making its current output unsuitable as historical as-known fundamentals. Preserve accession numbers and join values to their source filings. [SEC API documentation](https://www.sec.gov/search-filings/edgar-application-programming-interfaces).

The SEC FAQ states government-created content and public EDGAR filings are free to access and reuse. This is not a claim that issuer-authored material or every third-party exhibit is US-government-authored public-domain content. Respect identifiable third-party rights and avoid reproducing unnecessary documents in reports. [SEC reuse FAQ](https://www.sec.gov/about/webmaster-frequently-asked-questions).

**Research implication:** A financial period-end, filing date, filing acceptance time, API update time, and our first observation time describe different events. The first does not prove availability on that date. For precise historical reconstruction, preserve original filings, amendment links, and observed availability; mark unknown dissemination latency rather than backdating knowledge. Later comparative values can restate prior years.

## Financial ratios, valuation inputs and validation

The following are conceptual implementation requirements, not tested calculations or code:

- Derive ratios from selected reported facts with explicit accounting conventions. Keep a filing-specific lineage from numerator and denominator to accession, context, period, units and currency.
- Distinguish year-to-date cash flows from standalone quarter flows. A fourth quarter derived from annual less nine-month figures must use compatible accounting versions and be labeled as derived.
- For trailing earnings and cash flow, align fiscal periods and avoid adding cumulative values. Loss-making earnings, negative equity, discontinued operations and restatements require explicit ratio behavior.
- DCF requires cash-flow definitions, tax treatment, reinvestment, capital structure, share counts and discount-rate assumptions. Consensus estimates are optional external inputs, not reported results. Economic yields or locally estimated betas do not make a chosen risk premium a reported fact.
- Avoid dividing a latest market price by a future or restated earnings denominator in a historical valuation. Keep market observations and fundamentals joined by actual availability.
- Validate prices against corporate-action events and calendar/time-zone conventions. Massive's bars are split-adjusted by default; Tiingo documents split-and-dividend adjustment. Equal-looking adjusted-close columns can represent different return conventions. [Massive bar parameter](https://massive.com/docs/rest/stocks/aggregates/custom-bars), [Tiingo EOD conventions](https://www.tiingo.com/documentation/end-of-day).
- Cross-source checks should compare like-for-like raw versus adjusted values, currency, share class and venue before attributing a discrepancy to bad data. Check both sources' rights before saving comparison datasets; the Tiingo restriction above is particularly material.
- Treat provider completeness and error-checking claims as hypotheses for a later bounded sample: ordinary issuer, foreign filer, fiscal-year change, restatement, split, special dividend, renamed security, merger and bankruptcy. Record disagreements and unresolved cases.

## Important gaps

- No verified broad free PIT analyst-consensus archive, including historical revisions, analyst composition and timestamped forecast snapshots. Issuer guidance can be collected from filings but is not analyst consensus.
- No verified free complete global survivorship-aware daily-price/corporate-action/delisting-return database. Current tickers and surviving-company history cannot support claims of an unbiased historical universe.
- No unconditional free source for comprehensive transcript archives, deep intraday/tick history, borrow costs, institutional estimates, or international price coverage was established in this workstream.
- No account-level endpoint, data-quality, response-time or uptime benchmarking occurred. Documentation confirms published capabilities, not empirical quality.
- API limits, terms and plan entitlements can change. Capture reviewed terms and permitted provenance at onboarding and recheck before sharing, commercializing, or enabling autonomous use.

## Follow-up gates for Phase 2 planning

1. Confirm private individual/nonprofessional usage and select a small explicit market universe; none of the personal plans authorizes a future public commercial platform by default.
2. Start with primary reported fundamentals and lineage; validate extraction against original filings before introducing vendor-normalized ratios.
3. For price history, verify one no-card free account's exact entitlements and retention/non-display rights. Prefer explicit rights over larger advertised history. Stop at a card, paid upgrade, trial-only dataset or uncertain licence gate.
4. Collect a licensed, bounded price/corporate-action sample and perform independent numerical checks; retain both raw and adjusted conventions where allowed.
5. Establish future snapshots and identifier/event history. Do not relabel reconstructed latest data as a PIT archive.
6. Keep any analyst-estimate or international expansion optional until its free access and historical-availability evidence are sufficient.

No provider integration or implementation is authorized by these recommendations.
