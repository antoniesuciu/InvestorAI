# Milestone 3 — Market data provider decision

Verified **2026-10-28** for a private, personal, noncommercial application. This note supersedes the narrower price-only recommendation in [Phase 1](DATA_SOURCES.md#9-focused-stock-price-follow-up-personal-use) for Milestone 3's expanded corporate-action and reproducibility requirements. It does not change that historical research record.

**Current decision, 2026-10-29:** implement Alpaca Basic historical SIP first for the explicitly user-authorized private noncommercial application. A real read-only adapter, local versioned storage and [live validation](ALPACA_LIVE_VALIDATION.md) are delivered for review. Indefinite post-termination retention uncertainty alone no longer blocks implementation; [current access research](ALPACA_ACCESS.md) documents unverified contractual/account-feed conditions. This is not a perpetual archive, public redistribution, display or model-training license claim. HTTP restrictions, paid/recent feeds and automatic fallbacks remain disabled.

The comparison and initial decision below preserve the earlier research rationale; the current implementation scope above supersedes its offline-only delivery boundary.

## Initial 3A decision (historical)

**At 3A delivery, Alpaca Basic was the conditional preferred provider and delivery used provider-independent offline fixtures.** Alpaca's published raw/adjusted bars, corporate-action API and US history are the best technical fit. Material account/feed and retention conditions remain unresolved, so no live provider is declared approved merely because an API key exists. Twelve Basic remains a useful daily-price alternative, but does not include the independently inspectable action ledger needed for a complete replacement.

This decision follows the user's explicit fallback instruction: complete offline infrastructure when material access/licensing conditions remain unresolved. No account was created, credentials inspected, authenticated endpoint called, payment supplied or API data quality measured during this research. A published capability is distinguished below from verified account entitlement. Paid plans, welcome credits, demo-only symbols and trials are not durable free access.

**Alpaca's material unresolved access conditions:** the paper-only guide expressly says IEX-only, while the general historical FAQ permits older SIP requests without a subscription. Its general terms PDF contains US-residents-only wording, despite globally available paper registration. Incorporated customer/third-party agreements may change applicability. Do not resolve these contradictions by assuming the more permissive page wins. Ask the provider to establish eligibility and the exact feed for this user's account/jurisdiction; there is no authorized account creation or provider contact in this work. [Paper guide](https://docs.alpaca.markets/us/docs/paper-trading), [FAQ](https://docs.alpaca.markets/us/docs/market-data-faq), [terms](https://files.alpaca.markets/disclosures/library/TermsAndConditions.pdf).

EODHD is the justified third comparison because its terms expressly permit qualifying individuals to store/manipulate/analyze privately and its free plan includes actions. However, its same terms broadly restrict display/access/redistribution, and do not establish post-termination retention or ML-artifact rights. Combined with one-year history and indicative-data disclaimers, it does not resolve the complete use case. [Terms](https://eodhd.com/financial-apis/terms-conditions).

## Financial and operational implications

- **No source certifies our historical knowledge.** Bar session dates and action effective dates are not retrieval/publication dates. Neither provider's current adjusted history is an as-published vintage archive. Preserve retrieved bytes, request mode/feed, hashes, observed-at and revision links wherever retention permits. Missing availability remains unknown.
- Alpaca's `asof` bar parameter resolves symbol identity/name changes; it is **not** a historical data-availability cutoff. Explicitly record feed and adjustment rather than relying on defaults. [Bars](https://docs.alpaca.markets/us/reference/stockbars).
- Alpaca actions are filtered/sorted by **process date**, not automatically by ex-date. Its default excludes incomplete, not-yet-processed events; the provider expressly gives no creation-time guarantee. An empty response cannot prove no dividend/split occurred. [Actions](https://docs.alpaca.markets/us/reference/corporateactions-1).
- Incremental bars need an overlapping correction window and periodic historical reconciliation: new splits/dividends can revise much older adjusted bars. Preserve older observations; do not overwrite them or certify a correction horizon the provider does not promise. Action process-date polling needs its own cursor and revision handling.
- Do not replace missing sessions with synthetic prices or zero volume. Retain the calendar/session definition, local timezone and expected close separately. Holidays, suspensions, pre-IPO periods and failed retrieval are different states.
- Neither public documentation nor symbol lookup establishes complete delisted coverage, terminal payouts, historical security ownership or a survivorship-free universe. A small AAPL/MSFT watchlist is explicitly selected, not a historical investment universe.
- No provider listed supplies a verified free, point-in-time historical share-count series under this decision. Market capitalization and valuation must remain unavailable unless class, actual outstanding shares, split basis, dates, currency and reporting availability are independently compatible. Weighted-average diluted shares are not actual outstanding shares.

## Current implementation boundary and unresolved rights

The latest user instruction authorizes private local historical-data persistence subject to applicable provider terms, without requiring confirmation of indefinite post-termination retention before implementation. Alpaca is now implemented first, using read-only Basic historical SIP, separate raw/split/dividend representations, independent action process polling and observed asset/calendar evidence. The [live validation report](ALPACA_LIVE_VALIDATION.md) records controlled AAPL/MSFT results; original datasets stay in ignored local storage.

The historical-SIP FAQ and successful authenticated samples provide positive technical evidence. Conflicting paper-only IEX wording and unreviewed accepted subscriber/account agreements remain uncertainties, not established contractual clearance. Follow explicit restrictions and authorization failures; never bypass them or assume an upgrade. Account eligibility, retention/deletion on termination and future display/redistribution/ML rights remain separate review items. No provider contact, account modification, trading or paid fallback was performed. Private authorization does not authorize public publication of licensed datasets.

The previous offline/replay adapters remain supported. Other providers, complex corporate actions, certified past vintages, complete security-master/delisting handling, total returns and share-count/valuation integration remain outside this delivery. Missing evidence must remain visible rather than being manufactured.
